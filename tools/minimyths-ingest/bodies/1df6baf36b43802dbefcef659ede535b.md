# Description
The Grand Tournament is a few short weeks away. It's going to be *massive*, and it's a great opportunity for Victoria to defend their homeland!
This event is all about getting reps in before the GT, so we'll be running standard Showdown Premiere, with standard swiss pairings.
We'll have a few small door prizes available on the day, too!

### What you'll need:
A Showdown-Premiere list (that's four squads), (you are not required to have painted models for this event, however some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list), along with Dice, Rulers, Cards, and Tokens. If you're missing any of these, get in touch and we'll provide them on the day.

### Order of the Day:
- 10:30 AM - Guf Opens
- 11:00 AM - Round 1 will begin
- 1:00 PM - Round 1 complete, let's go for lunch!
- 1:30 PM - Round 2 will begin
- 3:30 PM - Round 3 will begin
- 5:30 PM- Round 3 complete, we'll hand out some prizes!

The final 30 minutes of each round will follow the Mission Critical rule.

### Player Standards:
All players should endeavor to create a fun, friendly environment for their opponent. [You can find our player standards here.](https://www.mini-myths.com/events/cities-under-siege#)
