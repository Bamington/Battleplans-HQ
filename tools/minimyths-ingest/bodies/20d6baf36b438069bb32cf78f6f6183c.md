# Summary
Hey all. Come down to Guf Werribee for our next infinity N5 Event. We will be hosting a 3 round, 300-point resilience operations event on the day.
Tickets will be $35, and every player will receive 4 HVT markers, 3 Objectives and 2 beacons, so you will have all the items needed to play resilience operations

# Players Pack
### What you will need to play:
- Good sportsmanship, patience and good communication skills.
- Appropriate miniatures to represent your army.
- Appropriate tokens to keep track of your orders, prone, unconscious, wounds, etc.
- A set of D20 dice (not 'spin downs')
- A set of templates (small and large teardrop and circular) and a ruler.
- A printout of your army lists and a "courtesy list" for your opponents.

### Structure of the event
This is a 300pts/6swc casual event, playing 3 rounds of Resilience Operations from the current ITS season 16 mission pack. Please also note that the Stealth Operations mid-season event rules will be in use for this event. For players that do not currently own an Operations Deck, this can either be purchased prior to the day, or alternatively, please reach out to the event organisers for a possible solution.
There is no minimum painting requirement, however, some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list.
Proxies are allowed, so long as the models share the same silhouette size and you are clear with your opponent what that model is meant to represent, including weaponry.

### Useful Links and Resources
Infinity N5 rules, N5 tokens, FAQs and other documents can be located on the official Infinity website - https://infinitytheuniverse.com/resources/infinity
Infinity Army Builder - https://infinitytheuniverse.com/army/infinity – note, there is also a phone app that can be downloaded, but this does not have printing functionality.
For help with the Army Builder, refer to this YouTube video - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGa4YP5MDWE
When you have finalised your army list, and are looking to print this off, you will see a number of different buttons on the webpage – the smiley face between 'save' and 'print' will let you print out a "courtesy list" to provide to your opponent.
