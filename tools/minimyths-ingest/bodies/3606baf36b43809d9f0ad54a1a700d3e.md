Nemesis Ops is here! To celebrate this exciting new narrative-driven format for Kill Team, we've prepared a special one-day event at Guf where you get to play both the Nemesis and the Hunted!

You'll bring your Kill Team of choice, alongside a model to use as a Nemesis! Don't worry - we'll have spare Kill Teams and spare Nemesises (Nemesi? Nemesis's?) for you to borrow if you need, too.

### What you need
- Any Kill Team of your choice, and an accompanying Nemesis (see below)
- All the usual Kill Team tokens, dice, rulers, etc.

# Building your Team
## Creating a Nemesis
**We'll complete Nemesis Creation together on the day** - however, you can start thinking about what model you'll bring with the following guidance:

### Size
Determine which size best suits your Nemesis Operative. As an example, an XV8 is small, and an Armoured Sentinel is Medium. Don't bring anything with a base over 100mm.

| Size | Control* | Move | Save | Wounds | Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small (S) | 4 | 6" | 4+ | 35 | 2 |
| Medium (M) | 5 | 6" | 4+ | 50 | 2 |
| Large (L) | 6 | 6" | 4+ | 75 | 3 |

*Control is a new stat for Nemesis operatives that's used for their APL stat, but has other functions too.*

### Weapons and Traits
We'll have the full list available on the day - but consider how many weapons your operative will have (based on it's size). It will also get three traits - one is decided by it's faction, and the two others are unique to it.

## Your Kill Team
You'll bring a standard Kill Team with the usual number of models, alongside a Nemesis that we'll construct rules for on the day. **The size and power of your Nemesis Operative determines how many models you take with you,** as per the table below:
**Bring your full complement on the day so you can change up your squad between rounds.**

| Kill Team's Normal # of Operatives | Small Nemesis | Medium Nemesis | Large Nemesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| 6 | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| 7 | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| 8 | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| 9 | 6 | 5 | 3 |
| 10 | 7 | 5 | 3 |
| 11 | 7 | 6 | 4 |
| 12 | 8 | 6 | 4 |
| 13 | 9 | 7 | 4 |
| 14 | 9 | 7 | 5 |

> For example, if you're using a **Small** Nemesis Operative, and your Kill Team is usually a **10 Operative** Kill Team, you'd bring **7 Operatives and your Nemesis.**
