### It all comes down to this.
The final event of our 2025 league! Come down and play some games, earn your final points, and send-off the year with an awesome celebration of a great year of Shatterpoint.
We've partnered up with Victoria's Mightiest Heroes and their Masters event, so we'll share a venue, share a meal, and share a great day of gaming!

This event will be a **Showdown Premiere** format, but as always, new players are welcome to play a standard **Showdown** format if they would like.

> **Outer-Rim League**
> This event is part of the **Outer Rim League!** From now until December, earn progress on a League Leaderboard by attending each of our Shatterpoint events. At the end of the season, we'll crown our champions and hand over some delicious exclusive bounty for our top-earners.

### What you'll need:
A Showdown Premiere list (that's four squads), along with Dice, Rulers, Cards, and Tokens. If you're missing any of these, get in touch and we'll provide them on the day.
You are not required to have painted models for this event, however some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list.

### Order of the Day:
- 9:00 - Venue Opens
- 9:45 - Round 1
- 11:45 - Lunch
- 12:30 - Round 2
- 2:30 - Round 3
- 4:30 - Wrap up and **Prizes!**

The final 30 minutes of each round will follow the Mission Critical rule.

### Player Standards:
All players should endeavor to create a fun, friendly environment for their opponent. [You can find our player standards here.](https://mini-myths.com/event-standards)
