# Purple Palette 6: Monster Madness

## Restrictions
Paint a **single miniature representing a monster, no taller than 30cm**. Yes, that excludes Archaon. The height limit includes any base or plinth.
- **Retail miniatures only.** Your miniature must come from a retail manufacturer (GW, AMG, Warlord, etc.). 3D-printed miniatures are only allowed if bought already printed from a retail manufacturer.
- **Accessories:** 3D-printed and sculpted accessories (heads, weapons, adornments) are allowed.
- **Basing:** Optional and completely freeform, so go nuts with it. You may mount your piece on a base or a plinth, as long as the total height stays at or under 30cm. Basing is one of the judging categories, so an unbased entry will likely score lower there.
- **Transfers:** Allowed.
- **Backdrops:** Allowed, but you must disclose if AI was used to generate the image.
- **Nameplates:** Entries are judged anonymously. You're welcome to add a nameplate with a title. It's your only chance to tell people about your work.

## The Spirit of the Event
- **Have fun!** Pick something you'll enjoy painting.
- This is an **amateur** painting competition. Treat it as a chance to experiment and learn.

## Judging Criteria
Your piece will be judged by the community and a team of judges using the standard Purple Palette criteria. Each category is scored out of 10, for a **total of 50 points**.
- **Brushwork & Execution:** Technical skill. Judges look for smooth blends, sharp edge highlights, precise Non-Metallic Metal (NMM) or True Metallic Metal (TMM), and no unintended brush strokes or texture.
- **Colour Theory & Scheme:** How the colours work together, including contrast (light vs dark, warm vs cool), saturation, and how the scheme draws the eye to the focal point.
- **Basing & Environment:** The base should complement the miniature, not distract from it. Judges look for cohesive environmental storytelling, realistic textures (mud, water, pigments) and a cleanly finished rim.
- **Creativity & Conversions:** Imagination. This covers freehand, kitbashing (combining parts from different kits) and scratch-built elements that take the model beyond its stock pose.
- **Craftsmanship & Cleanliness:** Build quality. Judges will deduct points for visible mould lines, gaps, or leftover sprue marks.

## Tips
- **Choose your miniature carefully.** Bigger isn't always better. A larger model can make imperfections easier to spot.
- **Pick something you enjoy painting.** It'll keep you engaged through the long haul.
- **Share your progress.** Feedback helps, and it can get you through the less fun parts.
