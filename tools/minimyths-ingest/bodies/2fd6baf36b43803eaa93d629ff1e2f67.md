## Threemiere Format
For this event, we'll be playing **THREEMIERE** - a popular fan-made format that asks you to bring a total of three squads, **using each one at least once** throughout the day!
This format uses all the rules and regulations of a Showdown Premiere event, with the following changes:
- Your Strike Team will consist of **Three Squads** using the normal affiliation and list-building rules.
- Each Squad must be used in at least one of your matches during the event.

### Shatterpoint Rules
We'll be using the most up to date Star Wars: Shatterpoint ruleset, including the May 2025 unit updates. All games will be played in the Showdown Premiere format, as specified in the official document. This includes the **Mission Critical** rule.
Any official Shatterpoint product with a release date before the date of the event will be considered eligible to be played in the event.
**Please note the following important rules:**
> You must have a physical copy (printed or official) of all your Unit, Stance, and Mission cards.
> Your Order cards must be official AMG mission cards (not printouts). If your order deck contains foiled cards, your order deck must be sleeved.
> You must have a physical printout of the most recent cards for that Unit, such as those in the 2025 Unit Update document.
> You must have the official model for each of your characters. You may use a proxy in your games if that proxy is approved by a Tournament Organiser. Upon request from either the TO or your Opponent, you must remove that proxy and replace it with the official model.

# Prizes
## Arcpoint Champion
Finishing at the top of the table will award you the **Arcpoint Champion** title, along with a fantastic custom trophy worth of a true champion.
Additionally, we'll have a suite of prizes for all our top-placed winners based on the event results.

## Champion of the Republic
Arcfest celebrates sportsmanship and fun above all else.
Across the day, you'll have the chance to submit feedback on how much fun each of your games were. At the end of the day, we'll award special prizes to those players who've been voted excellent opponents who created memorable gaming experiences.

## Emperor Paintpatine
Arcfest is a hobby-driven event and home to the epic ArcOpen painting competition, so we'd be remiss to not celebrate the amazing hobby efforts of our community at this event.
We'll crown multiple hobby winners on the day, alongside a trophy for our best-painted award; aptly named **Emperor Paintpatine.**

## The Display Boardalorian
Display boards are so cool! I love seeing them at *Warhammer* events, at *Marvel Crisis Protocol* events, and so on. Man, I wish we had display boards at our events…
Oh wait! Now we will! Bring your Display Board to Arc and you'll receive a special prize, plus an additional bonus prize for the player who is voted to have the best display board at the event! Display boards are easy and fun to make - just grab a picture frame from K-Mart and you're already 2/3rd of the way there!
And, since we love our silly titles, you'll also be crowned…**The Displayboardalorian**.

## Door Prizes
Arcfest is for players of any ability, so we want to do everything we can to reward every attendee! Expect an awesome showbag of custom made doohickeys and prizes.
We'll have more information about this closer to the day, but to give you a tease; you can certainly expect to see some **new** custom unit cards like those from last year's Outer-Rim League…

## Terrain Raffle
Any player who brings a full table of painted terrain will be entered into the **Terrain Raffle**! At the end of the event, we'll draw names from the raffle and give out some bonus prizes to those players. We'll announce what those prizes are on the day!
You get one raffle ticket for each full table of terrain you bring. If you are eligible to receive a raffle ticket, you will get that ticket regardless of if the terrain ends up being used on the day or not.
All terrain must be painted and designed for use with Star Wars: Shatterpoint.
Sign up for the terrain raffle will be posted closer to the event!
