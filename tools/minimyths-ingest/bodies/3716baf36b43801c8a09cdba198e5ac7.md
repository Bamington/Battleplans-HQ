### What you'll need to play

How do you make an Incursion list? Well, you need 1000 points of models at 2 detachment points of detachments.

What does that mean? Right now, I don't really know! Keep an eye on the Discord and we'll all work together to make some legal lists for the day once the full rules are released!

You'll also want to bring dice and measurement tools - safe to say, we'll still need those in this edition!

Although this event is for learning, we also ask you to do the following:

- familiarise yourself with the new core rules when available,
- provide yourself with a method of accessing those rules on the day,
- show up with your list ready and completely built,
- bring all your needed supplies; something to score with, dice, measuring tape, etc.,

**If this is your first time, reach out and let us know - we'll make sure you've got what you need!**
