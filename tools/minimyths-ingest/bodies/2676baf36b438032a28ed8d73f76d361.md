# The Theme: Fluoro
> This Purple Palette is all about Fluoro and it's bright, high-contrast aesthetic! Show us your fluoro paint, vibrant colour and/or object source lighting. Consider being inspired by glow in the dark looks, neon signage, and fluorescent paints.

# Assemble, and then Paint Your Heart Out!
Based on feedback from the first Purple Palette event, we've made entering easier — **no requirement to purchase a brand new model this time!**
- You can use any miniature, as long as it meets requirements. Must be **unpainted or primed only** when entered.
- Base size: **<=100mm** and **<=130mm tall**. Must use included base if supplied. Overhang max **50%** of base size.
- No sculpting or 3D printing allowed. **Kitbashing and decals are allowed.**
- Entry form (on Mini Myths site) required with: photo of unpainted mini (with name & date), personal details, entry name, and media opt-in.
- Your piece must **represent the theme** in some way — this is the most important judging criteria.
- Unsure? Reach out on **Facebook or Discord**.

# Judging Criteria
Judges will score each category (10 points each, total = **50 points**):
- **Execution** — How do all elements work together? What is the overall result?
- **Technique** — How well are fundamental skills (blending, shading, highlighting, etc.) applied?
- **Character** — Does it have identity, "wow factor," or impact?
- **Theme Representation** — Is the theme clear and well-integrated?
- **Personal Style** — Does the contestant's own style come through?

# Feedback
- All entrants receive their **criteria scores** within one week.
- Contestants may contact organizers for **judges' written feedback**.

# Advice
- Decide on a setting; the *feel* is as important as technique.
- Tell a story — final effect matters more than method.
- Make your piece "speak for itself" (focal points: face, chest, weapons).
- Don't rely only on sculpted detail — creativity matters most.
- Size isn't everything — even 25mm models can impress.
- Choose a model you're passionate about.
- Share progress with friends for feedback.
- Above all — **have fun!**

# Prizes
Prizes reward more entrants across multiple categories:
- **Top Score** — Highest average judge score.
- **Most Improved** — Biggest improvement vs. previous Purple Palette entry.
- **Community Vote** — Attendees pick their three favourite entries.
- **Best Character** — Strongest "wow factor" and execution.
- **Best Newcomer** — Highest scoring first-time entrant.
- **Best Under 18's** — Highest score in youth entries.

*Note: Each entrant can only win one prize. If eligible for multiple, they receive the most valuable prize, and a runner-up is chosen for the other.*
