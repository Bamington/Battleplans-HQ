# Description
Sadly, Arcpoint has been cancelled. However, don't lose hope (after all, rebellions are built on hope) - instead, come celebrate May 4th in style at Gaming Arena!
This event is all about getting reps in before the GT, so we'll be running standard Showdown Premiere, with standard swiss pairings.
We'll have a few small door prizes available on the day, too!

### The Rebalance
There's a small chance that the rebalanced rules will come out before this event. I will announce in the Discord and through a Longshanks message if we're using the new rules, so keep an eye out!

### Star Wars Trivia
GA are also running Star Wars trivia that night, at 6:00 PM! We'll finish up just in time to join the festivities for those who'd like to do so. [Details can be found here](https://www.thegamingarena.com.au/product/star-wars-may-the-4th-trivia-/11505).

### What you'll need:
A Showdown-Premiere list (that's four squads), (you are not required to have painted models for this event, however some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list), along with Dice, Rulers, Cards, and Tokens. If you're missing any of these, get in touch and we'll provide them on the day.

### Order of the Day:
- 9:00 AM - Gaming Arena Opens
- 10:00 AM - Round 1 will begin
- 12:15 AM - Round 1 complete, let's go for lunch!
- 1:00 PM - Round 2 will begin
- 3:15 PM - Round 3 will begin
- 5:30 PM- Round 3 complete, we'll hand out some prizes!

The final 30 minutes of each round will follow the Mission Critical rule.

### Player Standards:
All players should endeavor to create a fun, friendly environment for their opponent. [You can find our player standards here.](https://www.mini-myths.com/events/cities-under-siege#)
