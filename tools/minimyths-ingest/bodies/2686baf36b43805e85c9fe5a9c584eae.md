# Spots for this event are highly limited! Get yours before they're sold out!

It can be hard to find the time to pick up a whole new game, let alone three - but hey, we've found a solution! Come along to our Learn to Play day! For this event, we're focusing on some of our favourite skirmish games: **Kill Team**, **Warcrow**, and **Halo: Flashpoint**!

Even if you've got experience in one or two of the games we have on display, we think you'll have a great time! When you're playing a game you're familiar with, flex your mentoring muscles and help a beginner discover the love of the game!

## Format of the Day
You'll play three games on the day - one of each game. The order is somewhat random, and you'll be paired up with a fellow learn-to-player. Each table has a dedicated teacher there to help get you started and teach you the system, as well as to answer any questions you might have.
Each table will have some beginner-friendly reference cards that you can take home with you after the event, too. **You don't need to bring anything to this event -** we'll provide everything you need to play.

### Kill Team
Play an awesome small-sided game using some beginner-friendly Kill Teams. If you've been holding off on checking out Kill Team, this is a great chance to give it a go!

### Warcrow
Experience the introductory scenario to Warcrow's latest release, Song of the Dormant! Check out a few of the Warcrow factions and learn about this incredibly unique skirmish game from Corvus Belli.

### Halo: Flashpoint
Classic Halo - you're playing Slayer! A quick-paced 4v4 battle with respawns, weapon pickups, and all the Halo flavour you can ask for.

## FAQ's
TOGGLE-Q: Can I bring my own miniatures?
TOGGLE-A: You're welcome to bring your own miniatures for any of the three games, however depending on the scenario, we might ask you to use ours instead. We'd love for you to show them off and share your love for the game, though!

TOGGLE-Q: I'm an expert in one of the games, but I'd like to try the others. Can I come?
TOGGLE-A: Definitely! We'd ask that you use your time with the game you know well to really help out your opponent and teach them about what you love about the game.
