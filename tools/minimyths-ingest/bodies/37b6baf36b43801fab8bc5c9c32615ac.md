## League Structure
This league will be run as per the core manual with a few adjustments as described in this document. This league will be run with a **single division**. We will hold a public fixture at **minimyths.com**.
The time limit for games will be the week they are scheduled for. If you are unable to play your opponent due to life commitments, you can schedule your games in for either **Week 6** or **Week 12**. These weeks are reserved for catch up games.
You may instead choose to **concede**. In which case your opponent will receive a **1-0 Win** and **D6 x 10,000 gold** and pick **two MVPs** from their roster.
> If you cannot make your game in the assigned period or do not schedule a reserve game and choose not to concede, you will be docked **3 League Points**. This is to address players who are trying to avoid certain opponents. Please communicate with your opponent about the likelihood of your game.

## The Fixture
**Weeks 1 and 7** are both **Grudge Rounds**. If you have a particular coach or team you wish to issue a grudge to, please inform the TO. Once a player has accepted a grudge they will be unable to issue one themselves. Use grudge matches to ensure you get to play a particular player in the league in the case of a large player count.
Otherwise, teams will be paired initially on team tiers to allow for the best experience in the initial rounds. The team tier list can be found in the FAQ and Errata document for Warhammer Community.
Each coach will play **10 games** on the fixture. Depending on player counts a coach may end up playing the same team twice. At the end of the fixture, we will hold a Play Offs for the finals (the size will be determined by total player count). The fixture will be public on the Mini-Myths website as well as through Death Pitch.

### The Play Offs
After the fixture we will hold a Play Offs. Depending on player count this will either be **top four (4)** or **top eight (8)**. The play offs will be a single day event. Losses will result in player knock outs.

### Submitting Results
Results will be submitted through **DeathPitch**. This will also allow you to track your scores and team's progress. Along with results, please submit your petty cash calculations and spending for auditing purposes by the **Blood Bowl League Accounting Association (BBLAA)**.

## Player Expectations
Remember that this is a game of fun and dice. Players are expected to play their opponent in a fun and friendly manner. Please be honest when recording results and tracking player outcomes. Any negative play, such as not engaging with your opponent to try and farm SPP, should be reported to the TO. Anyone in breach of these standards will be docked **3 League points** from their fixture (the same value as a Win).
This event will also be following the **Mini-Myths Community Expectations**. Please note that any breaches of these expectations will result in a **strike**. If you accrue **three or more strikes** then disciplinary action will be taken that may result in removal from the event, a temporary or permanent ban from future Mini-Myth events. There is a full list of community expectations regarding behaviour located on the Mini-Myths website.

## Painting Requirements
There is no requirement for your team to be painted at the start of the league. However, **not having your team fully painted by the Play Offs will revoke your eligibility for some hobby related prizes.**

B4M is a custom star player! No one knows what is under that barrel. All they know is that he is sturdy, bounces and sometimes rolls! B4M has the following profile:

| MA | ST | AG | PA | AV | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 3 | 3+ | 5+ | 9+ | 170,000 GP |

**Skills & Traits:** Thick Skull, Dodge, Steady Footing, Pro, Fend, Stunty, Loner (4+)
**Special Rule — 99 Barrels of B4M:** Once per game, if this player would be knocked down, either from a block or from tripping and falling over, you may use this ability. ## Special Rules
### Highlander Rule
The Highlander rule, AKA **"There can be only one!"** applies to all Star Players.
If opposing coaches have selected the same Star Player, the Highlander rule will apply. The Star Player storms off muttering *"There can be only one!"*, meaning neither team is allowed to field the Star Player in question. The Star Player will sit out the entire match.
> *"Why do you think there are 99 of him?"*
**Note that B4M is exempt from this rule.**
You can find the reference for this rule at https://www.bigvbloodbowl.com/roster-rules
