# How to Enter
## Purchase a Model
To get started, you'll need to grab a brand-new model from **Guf Werribee**. Your model should comply with the following restrictions:
- This is a single model entry — no squads, companions, or familiars. Small vehicles (cavalry, bikes, etc.) are eligible.
- Model must fit on a base no larger than 65mm, or be free-standing. Plinths/dioramas not allowed.
- Overhang is fine (spears, wings, etc.) as long as the majority fits within a 65mm footprint.
- No kitbashing, 3D printing, or sculpting. Only use parts/decals that come with the kit.
- Unsure? Reach out on Facebook or Discord for clarification.

## Assemble and Paint
This is a painting competition — so paint your heart out!
- Share progress on **Werribee Tabletop Facebook** or **Mini Myths Discord**.
- Ask for tips, advice, and feedback.
- Hobby is about learning and growing together!

## Stand and Be Judged
- **Deadline:** On or before **06/04/24**, drop your entry at Guf Werribee.
- All entries will be displayed on judging day.
- Judges will score each entry, and prizes will be awarded.

# Advice
- **Decide on a setting for your piece.** The "feel" of the model is just as important as the technical approach.
- **Tell a story.** While the judges will critique your technical abilities, the use of any painting technique is not considered poor. Remember it is not how you paint it, just the end result that the judges will see.
- **Make sure your piece can "speak for itself".** You will not have the opportunity to communicate details to the judges. Pay attention to natural focal points like the face, chest and any weapons/tools.
- **Sculpted details are not the be all and end all.** Your ability to tell a story through your piece and the way you approach it is much more important.
- **Does size matter?** Painting technique and creativity are key! A 25mm piece can show even more than a 65mm piece in the right hands.
- **Show your passion.** Pick a piece you are very passionate about. This helps to keep focus when you eventually reach that "I hate this" stage.
- **Share your work with friends.** Having another set of eyes to look over your piece can be helpful when you are unsure of where to take it next.
- **Remember to have fun!** This is ultimately a chance to share your skills and style for a hobby we all love.

# FAQs
TOGGLE-Q: When is the cut-off point?
TOGGLE-A: You can sign up any time during the six weeks. We recommend starting within the first two weeks.
TOGGLE-Q: Do I need to paint a Warhammer model?
TOGGLE-A: No — any brand/range is fine if purchased via Guf Werribee and follows restrictions.
TOGGLE-Q: Is there a minimum spend?
TOGGLE-A: No minimum spend required.
TOGGLE-Q: Is there a minimum painting standard?
TOGGLE-A: No enforcement, but we recommend the "Strict" standard: at least three colours, painted base.
TOGGLE-Q: Do I have to paint purple?
TOGGLE-A: No — no theme colours required.
TOGGLE-Q: What if the model doesn't come with a base?
TOGGLE-A: It must still fit within a 65mm footprint. Contact TOs if unsure.
TOGGLE-Q: Can I mount my model on an alternative base?
TOGGLE-A: Yes, but the base must match the intended size of the supplied base.
TOGGLE-Q: What happens if I don't follow guidelines?
TOGGLE-A: Your piece won't be eligible for prizes. Check with TOs if unsure.
TOGGLE-Q: Can I buy a kit with multiple models and enter one?
TOGGLE-A: Yes — you may enter a single model from a multi-kit box, but customization must use only parts from that model.
TOGGLE-Q: Can I use bits from other kits for basing?
TOGGLE-A: No — only tufts/flock/basing materials allowed. Exception: plastic basing kits (Citadel Skulls/Vines) are fine.
TOGGLE-Q: What are judges looking for?
TOGGLE-A: Each judge has their own criteria. For tips, see Mindful Painting Tips.
