Welcome to our **very first event at Top Table Games!**

This event will be in the **Threemiere** format, but as always, new players are welcome to play a standard **Showdown** format if they would like.

### What you'll need:
A Threemierelist (that's three squads), along with Dice, Rulers, Cards, and Tokens. If you're missing any of these, get in touch and we'll provide them on the day.
You are not required to have painted models for this event, however some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list.
