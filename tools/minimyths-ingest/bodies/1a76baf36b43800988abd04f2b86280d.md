# Description
It's been a while, but Age of Sigmar is back, baby! Let's get metaphysical with a full-force 2000-point event to kick off April!

## What You Will Need to Play
- Good sportsmanship, patience, and good communication skills
- Appropriate miniatures to represent your army
- A set of D6 dice
- A tape measure or range rulers
- Appropriate rules documents (GHB, core rules, battletome, etc.)

## Structure of the Event
This is a **2000pt matched play event**.
- There is **no minimum painting requirement**, however, some prizes may only be eligible for players with a **fully painted list**
- **Proxies are allowed**, provided:
  - They are approved by the TO prior to the event
  - You clearly communicate what the model represents, including weaponry, to your opponent

## Timing
The event will commence at **10:00am**, with the following round structure:
- **Doors open:** 10:00am
- **Round 1 – Shifting Priorities:** 10:30am
- **Lunch:** 12:30pm
- **Round 2 – Limited Resources:** 1:00pm
- **Round 3 – Focal Points:** 3:00pm
