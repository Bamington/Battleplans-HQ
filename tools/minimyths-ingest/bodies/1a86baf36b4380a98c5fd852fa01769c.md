# Welcome to the War for Werribee, 2025.

This event is a Victoria's Mightiest Heroes qualifier! Earn points, or even a place, in the VMH masters event later this year.

Mini Myths is excited to be collaborating with the best of Victoria's MCP events to continue **Victoria's Mightiest Heroes** - the premiere competitive MCP circuit for Victoria. This year, we're the final event before the final - so come grab some last minute points and earn your spot!

We've learned plenty from last year's event, and we've made some big improvements this year - most excitingly, we've hired our own space to run the event at Arndell Park Community Center! This means more space for us to play in, and room to welcome many, many more players than last year.

We hope you'll join us once again for the **War for Werribee!**

# Rules
## Scoring
Scoring and tiebreaking will follow the official format as laid out in the most recent competitive document (see below).
Players are able to submit their scores themselves via Longshanks. TO's are available to submit scores, but only if players are unable to do so.
**The player submitting the game result should *always* do so together with their opponent.**
## Rules
We'll be using the most up to date Marvel Crisis Protocol ruleset, including the most recent errata and updates. All games will be played in the Challanger format, as specified in the official document.
**Please note the following important rules:**
> You must have a physical copy (printed or official) of all your Unit, Tactics, and Crisis Cards.
> Your cards must be the most recent version as per the AMG website.
> You must have the official model for each of your characters. You may use a proxy in your games if that proxy is approved by a Tournament Organiser. Upon request from either the TO or your Opponent, you must remove that proxy and replace it with the official model.
