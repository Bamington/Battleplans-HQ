# Welcome to our very first Stumble Rumble!

Welcome to Stumble Rumble—Mini Myth's premier (and possibly only) Blood Bowl Sevens amateur league, hosted proudly at Guf Werribee!

In this league, strategy meets chaos, dice meet destiny, and rookies and useless veterans alike gather each week to indulge in the finest tradition Blood Bowl has to offer; spectacular triumphs, brutal fumbles, and the occasional goblin-related incident.

The Stumble Rumble delivers fast-paced Sevens play—shorter halves, tighter pitches, and just enough players to keep things competitive without preventing the mayhem we all love. Whether you're here to hone your coaching skills, test a fresh team, or simply watch someone fail a dodge roll they absolutely should have made, you'll find a place in our dugouts.

So lace up your boots, strap on your questionable armour, and get ready. The whistle's blown, the crowd's roaring and at Guf Werribee… everyone stumbles, but only the bold rumble.

## League Structure

- The league will commence on 18th January.
- The fixture will run for 10 games over 12 weeks with both the sixth and twelfth week being reserved for a single catch up game each.
- You will be provided with your full fixture at the commencement of the league.

If you are unable to meet your opponent for your weekly game, you must concede for the week.

If both players are unable to attend the game than one must concede. This should be agreed to in a gentlemanly manner or be resolved by a dice roll. No ties will be offered for missed games even if both players agree to it.

For each five-week game block, two players who are unable to have their game may mutually agree to reserve their game for the catch up week. Each player pair is only allowed one catch up game per block so please ensure that is the game you want to postpone. If you already have a postponed game for the five week block than any other games you want to postpone you must concede.

Players without a catch up game for the sixth and twelfth weeks will be allowed to compete in a friendly match. This match will not advance team progression and is considered an exhibition match for your team.

After the 12 week fixture we will hold a playoffs. The size of which will be determined by total player count.
