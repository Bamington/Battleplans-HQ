### Rules to note:
- This will be two rounds of 1000 point Chapter Approved 2025-26 missions and any tournament companion rules that come out alongside.
- Missions will be determined by our TO on the day (with twists)
- We'll be treating bottom floor windows as boarded up.
- Apply a battle ready 10 point bonus regardless of if you are painted to battle ready standard. Just have your models completely assembled.

### Player conduct:
- Please make sure you have a reasonable enough grip on the core rules and some familiarity with your army. This event will be chill so we aren't expecting mastery though.
- Bring everything you need to play (rules, measuring tapes, etc).
- Don't be mean or disrespectful, discrimination won't be permitted.
- Wish your TO a happy birthday.

### Timetable:
- Round 1: 10:45am to 1:45pm
- 15 minute minimum break: 1:45pm to 2:00pm
- Round 2: 2:00 to 5:00pm
- Pack up and raffle: 5:00pm to 5:30pm

Standings will be based on wins/losses, then points.

$10 entry. A portion of this entry fee will go into Mini Myths' club funds, with the rest going straight to the prize pool!
Please note that prize support will be all door prizing that is raffled off, depending on what is available to raffle.
