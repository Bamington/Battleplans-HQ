Halo Flashpoint is the awesome new Halo-themed game from Mantic, and we think it's **gonna be big!** It's tabletop skirmish like you've never played before, with unique twists from the video game like picking up weapons on the battlefield, respawning when you die, and more!

Fiesta is a unique multiplayer gamemode that allows players to join a free-for-all Deathmatch! Spawn in, and get as many kills as you can before you're fragged! It's a fun, frenetic mode that's super fast to play and very easy to learn - we think you'll love it!

Not only that, but we'll **provide everything you need to play!**

You're welcome to bring your own models from home, but if you haven't picked up Flashpoint yet, you can still easily play in this event.

After Round 1, if players are keen to try the full Halo Flashpoint Experience, you'll also have the option of pairing up and playing a full game!

### Event Structure
This is an introductory Flashpoint event, for people of varying experience levels, and equally open to people that are Flashpoint-curious.
For those that do not own a copy of Flashpoint, everything will be provided for you on the day.
For those that own a copy of Flashpoint, you are more than welcome to bring your copy along and to use your own dice, tokens, etc.

The event will commence at 10:30am, with round times as follows:
- Doors open – 10:30am
- Round 1 commences – 11:00am
- Lunch – 12:30pm to 1:00pm
- Round 2 commences – 1:00pm
- Round 3 commences – 3:00pm
