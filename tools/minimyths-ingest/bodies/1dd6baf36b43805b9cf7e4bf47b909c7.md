# Description
A mysterious psychic energy calls out through the Warp, drawing the attention of all is the Western Sector of the galaxy.
At the centre of this psychic call is the solar system of Guf, where the ominous Chaeos Cult plots on the dwarf planet Mythos.
Join us for a 6-round matched play league, using the current mission pack, beginning on May 27th.
Format: whatever the current matched play mission pack is - if it's Pariah Nexus we will use that, if it's 2024-2025 we will use that. If the new pack releases during the first half of the league, we will switch over when we switch points (if viable).
Rounds 1 and 3 are Incursion (1000 points). Rounds 4 to 6 are Strike Force (2000 points)
Cost: $40

# Overview:
1. You are matched with an opponent every fortnight and have that fortnight to get your game in.
2. Each fortnight, the mission and pairings will be set by Chae and communicated out by the Mini Myths Discord and Longshanks, at approximately 5:30pm each fortnightly Tuesday.
3. Match results are due by 4:30pm each fortnightly Tuesday.
4. Based at Guf Werribee, but its not necessary to play there.
5. Standings will be based off of wins/losses, then point differentials, then strength of schedule.
6. You will need to submit your 1000 point list at the start of the league. You will be locked into this list for each Incursion game.
7. You will need to submit your 2000 point list at the start of round 4. You will be locked into this list for each Strike Force game.
8. Your two army lists do not have to have anything in common! For example, you could play Orks and then switch to Necrons for Strike Force.

# FAQs
**What if my opponent cancels/we can't get a game in?**
If one player is at fault for a cancelled game they will take a game loss with 0 points. The other player will be awarded a win as if they had 66 points. If you can't schedule a game in and it's neither player's fault, you can agree to a draw with 33 to 33 points.

**Can I change my list between rounds?**
No! Unless something big like a dataslate or new mission pack drops we'll keep things the same. Your 1000 point and 2000 points lists will be able to be completely different though. They don't even have to share the same super faction.

**How are we handling terrain?**
I would recommend you talk to your opponent about that and set things up as you see fit. We encourage though that you use the WTC layouts (https://worldteamchampionship.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/WTC-2025-Map-Pack-Lite-v1.2_compressed.pdf) and treat your bottom floor windows as blocking LOS. I've made some terrain footprint mats out of clear plastic and added them to various terrain sets at Guf Werribee to help facilitate this.

**How are standings handled?**
Your standings will primarily be based on W/L/D record. The next tie breaker will be point differentials (how much you beat your opponent by, so make sure to deny those last minute primary points). Then if needed strength of schedule is used. Essentially, you'll be rated slightly higher based on how strong your opponents were.

**Is this a narrative event?**
No. I'm sure we'll forge our own narratives through this, and tell some fun stories, but there is no narrative or crusade elements to this league. At its core, it's just straight up matched play. I'm sure I'll write some fun fluff about the Chaeos Cult but doing the same for your army is not a requirement nor an expectation.
