## Rules
- You must paint a miniature from a retail manufacturer (GW, AMG, Warlord, etc.). You may not use a 3D printed mini unless it is purchased from a retail manufacturer.
- The model must come with a supplied square/round base no bigger than 28mm in diameter.
- You may rebase the mini to another base or replace the base with a display plinth, but it must not exceed 35mm in width/diameter.
- 3D printed and sculpted accessories are accepted such as heads/weapons or adornments.
- Back drops are allowed for your miniature but you must disclose if AI was used to generate the image.
- Transfers are allowed.
- Your piece will be judged anonymously by the community and a team of judges.
- You are welcome to add a nameplate to your piece to give it a title. This is your only opportunity to communicate your work.
- Basing is completely freeform. Go nuts with it.
- Have Fun! Make sure you are going to paint something you will enjoy.
- This is an amateur painting competition. Please use this as an experience to experiment and learn from your work.

## Judging Criteria
We have some revised criteria for this specific Purple Palette. Each category is worth **10 points**, for a total possible tally of **50 points**.

| Category | Description | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brushwork and Execution | Evaluates technical mastery. Judges look for flawlessly smooth blends, sharp edge highlights, precise Non-Metallic Metal (NMM) or True Metallic Metal (TMM) effects, and the absence of brush strokes or unintended texture. | 10 |
| Color Theory and Scheme | Judges assess how colors interact. This includes contrast (light vs. dark, warm vs. cold), saturation, and how the color choices guide the viewer's eye to the miniature's focal point. | 10 |
| Basing and Environment | A model's base should complement the miniature rather than distract from it. Judges look for cohesive environmental storytelling, realistic textures (mud, water, pigments), and a cleanly finished rim (often painted black). | 10 |
| Creativity and Conversions | This rewards imagination. It covers unique freehand designs, creative kitbashing (combining parts from different kits), and scratch-built elements that elevate the model beyond its stock pose. | 10 |
| Craftsmanship and Cleanliness | The foundational build quality is crucial. Judges will immediately deduct points if they spot visible mold lines, plastic/resin gaps, or leftover sprue attachment marks. | 10 |
| **Total** |  | **50** |

The judges will give you a score for each of these categories. Each category is worth 10 points, giving you a total possible points tally of 50.

**Judging will take place on October 9th** (Guf Hobby Night). Anyone attending can community vote and judges will resolve feedback. Feedback to be supplied to contestants.
