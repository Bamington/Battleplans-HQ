# Description
The GT is over - and the journey to *the next GT* has just begun!
Somehow, Our monthly Shatterpoint at Gaming Arena has returned. And with it has come the might AMG League Kit! Featuring exclusive foil cards and other fun prizes.
From now until December, earn progress on a League Leaderboard by attending each Gaming Arena monthly. At the end of the season, we'll crown our champions and hand over some delicious exclusive bounty for our top-earners.
This month, we'll be doing the **Threemiere** format - you'll bring a total of **3 SQUADS**, and you **must** use each squad at least once over the course of the day.

> Are you new to Shatterpoint? We can provide everything you need to start playing, and best of all - first-time players at our Monthlies enter **for free!** Reach out to Bam on the Gaming Arena or Mini Myths Discord if you'd like to come along.

### What you'll need:
A Threemiere list (that's three squads).
You are not required to have painted models for this event, however some prizes may only be eligible for players with a fully painted list.
You'll need the usual Dice, Rulers, Cards, and Tokens. If you're missing any of these, get in touch and we'll provide them on the day.
