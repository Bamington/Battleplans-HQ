A few of us will be looking to go to Arc this year, so it is time to turn our minds to 1350 point lists. Whether or not you are going, it would be great if you could join us for three rounds of matched play!

Since part of the point of this is to start getting ready for a big event, we will have an alternative way for you to get battle ready points. You don't need to be "battle ready" painted to get them, but of course that would count too! You will of course still need to have your models fully assembled and on their correct bases however.

You can get your 10 points through this alternative: show us something you are doing/have done for your Arc prep. For example, show us your display board, your themed accessories or your army lore.

### Prizing:
- Vouchers for podium placements
- 3D printed goodies for all, picked in standing order
Standing order will be determined by W/L records, using point differentials as a second denominator.

### Timetable:
- Round 1: 11am to 12:45pm
- Lunch: 12:45pm to 1:15pm
- Round 2: 1:15pm to 3:00pm
- Round 3: 3:00pm to 4:45pm
- Prizes and packup: 4:45pm to 5:15pm
To help us make time, we would appreciate a hand with set up and pack up on the day but this is not mandatory.

### Missions:
- Round 1: Mission A (Take and Hold, Tipping Point)
- Round 2: Mission K (Scorched Earth, Search and Destroy)
- Round 3: Mission M (Purge the Foe, Crucible of Battle)

### Terrain:
We are using the terrain layouts from the Chapter Approved Tournament Companion. Half of our tables will be layout 1 and the other half will be layout 6, so we don't have to switch out terrain throughout the day.
First floor windows should be treated as though they are solid and can't be seen through.
